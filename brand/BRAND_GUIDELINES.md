# PeopleKit Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence

**PeopleKit** is a modern HR and employee toolkit for teams that need quick answers, cleaner workflows, and practical resources without the friction of bloated enterprise software.

**Tagline:** The ultimate free toolkit for HR and work

**Voice:** Clear, practical, human. Support real workplace decisions without unnecessary jargon.

## Logo

- **Mark:** Ascending beam paths forming a stylized "V" with a luminous apex—symbolizing clarity rising from complexity.
- **Clear space:** Minimum padding equal to the height of the inner triangle in the mark.
- **Minimum size:** 24px height (digital), 8mm (print).

## Color System

| Role | Light Mode | Dark Mode | Usage |
|------|------------|-----------|--------|
| Primary | Cyan 600 `#0891b2` | Cyan 400 `#22d3ee` | CTAs, links, focus rings |
| Accent | Violet 500 `#8b5cf6` | Violet 400 `#a78bfa` | Highlights, gradients |
| Highlight | Amber 500 `#f59e0b` | Amber 400 `#fbbf24` | Badges, alerts |
| Surface | Slate 50 `#f8fafc` | Slate 950 `#0b0f1a` | Backgrounds |
| Text | Slate 900 | Slate 100 | Body copy |

**Gradients:** `from-cyan-500 via-violet-500 to-amber-400` for hero accents only—never on body text.

## Typography

- **Display / Headings:** Instrument Sans (600–700)
- **Body / UI:** Plus Jakarta Sans (400–600)
- **Code / Numbers:** JetBrains Mono (tabular figures for calculators)

## Design Language

- **Glassmorphism:** `backdrop-blur-xl` + semi-transparent borders on cards and navigation.
- **Elevation:** Soft shadows; avoid harsh drop shadows.
- **Radius:** `rounded-xl` for cards, `rounded-2xl` for modals, `rounded-full` for pills.
- **Motion:** Framer Motion fades and subtle y-translate (8–16px); respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Iconography

- Lucide-style stroke icons at 1.5px, 20–24px default size.
- Category icons use primary color at 60% opacity on tinted backgrounds.

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast minimum for all text.
- Focus visible rings: 2px cyan offset.
- Never convey meaning by color alone.

## Don'ts

- Do not stretch or rotate the logo.
- Do not use generic stock "calculator" clipart.
- Do not use competitor-adjacent names (SalaryHub, HRTools, etc.).
